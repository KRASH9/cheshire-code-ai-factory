## Concept
The Cheshire Cat is alone in a vast desert, but he is not lost or afraid. He touches a small golden lantern and calmly looks toward the viewer as it illuminates. The light becomes a quiet symbol of inner company.

## Meta AI video prompt

Create a cinematic, photorealistic vertical 9:16 video, exactly 10 seconds long. Live-action-style mystical cinema, not a cartoon or animation.

**LOCKED CHARACTER:** one royal mystical Cheshire Cat with grey / silver fur (never blue), bright turquoise stripes and bright turquoise eyes. Preserve the exact same face, proportions, fur pattern and stripe placement throughout and across all videos. Regal, wise, calm and mysterious; never cute, cartoonish, comedic or Disney-like. Silver fur moves gently in the desert wind. Long calm confident gaze.

**LOCKED VOICE:** Cheshire himself speaks directly to the viewer in a deep British bass, slow, wise and calm, with Received Pronunciation (RP). The voice clearly comes from the cat's mouth. Natural English lip/muzzle movement synced to the single spoken line. No narrator, unseen voice, other speaker or dialogue.

**LOCKED WORLD:** vast desert dunes at twilight, visible sand, cinematic warm low light, purple and turquoise smoke, glowing particles and restrained golden sparks. Quiet, regal, mysterious.

**CAMERA:** one continuous smooth shot; slow tracking or gentle zoom only. No fast cuts, shaky camera or meme editing.

**EXACT 10-SECOND TIMING**
- **0–2s HOOK:** The Cheshire Cat reaches forward with one paw and touches a small unlit golden lantern resting in the sand. The action starts immediately; do not begin with a static portrait.
- **2–7s STORY:** The cat slowly raises his head and looks directly toward the camera. His expression is peaceful and confident. Desert wind gently moves his silver fur. No additional action.
- **7–8s TURN:** The lantern softly illuminates. A subtle turquoise and purple smoke ribbon curls around it with a few golden particles. Keep the effect small and physically coherent.
- **8–9s PHRASE:** Cheshire visibly moves his mouth naturally and speaks exactly: **“Alone doesn't mean unwanted.”** Deep British bass, Received Pronunciation, calm and clear. No other words.
- **9–10s SILENCE:** The cat remains still, looking peacefully toward the viewer. Only soft desert wind. No speech.

**PHYSICS:** if a shadow appears, it must be flat on the sand, attached to the cat's paws, perspective-correct and naturally stretched by the low light. Cat-shaped only. No human or humanoid shadows.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT:** blue fur, blue stripes, different cat face, changing markings, cartoon, cute kitten, Disney-like, human figure, humanoid shadow, floating shadow, detached shadow, extra animals, extra characters, narrator, second voice, multiple phrases, subtitles, text overlay, fast cuts, shaky camera, meme editing, watermark, logo.

One cat. One action. One magical moment. One spoken sentence. One second of silence.
