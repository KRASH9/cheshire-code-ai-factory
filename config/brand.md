## Locked video format
Vertical 9:16. Default 10 seconds. A 30-second video is exactly three coherent 10-second parts.

- 7–8s TURN: one small magical event (smoke, light or subtle bloom).
- 8–9s PHRASE: Cheshire directly speaks one original, concise English wisdom line.
- 9–10s SILENCE: one second of desert wind and stillness; no speech.

Never more than one spoken phrase per 10-second part. The line must fit naturally in one second; simplify it rather than speeding up the voice.
