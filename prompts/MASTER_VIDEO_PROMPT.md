# MASTER VIDEO PROMPT — THE SPEAKING CHESHIRE CAT

Use for every new concept. Fill only the story fields. Character, voice, world, physics and timing are locked.

## Story fields
- Concept / human truth: [one clear researched or evergreen idea]
- Hook action: [one visible action in the first two seconds]
- Cheshire's simple action: [one action only]
- Magical turn: [one subtle smoke / light event]
- Final wisdom line: "[one original short English sentence spoken by Cheshire]"
- Research note: [source and insight, if researched; never copied wording]

## Meta AI master prompt

Create a cinematic, photorealistic vertical 9:16 video, exactly 10 seconds long. Live-action-style mystical cinema, not a cartoon or animation.

**LOCKED CHARACTER:** one royal mystical Cheshire Cat with grey / silver fur (never blue), bright turquoise stripes and bright turquoise eyes. Preserve the exact same face, proportions, fur pattern and stripe placement throughout and across all videos. Regal, wise, calm and mysterious; never cute, cartoonish, comedic or Disney-like. Silver fur moves gently in the desert wind. Long calm confident gaze. The Cat looks toward the viewer when delivering the line.

**LOCKED VOICE:** Cheshire himself speaks in a deep British bass, slow, wise and calm, with Received Pronunciation (RP). The voice clearly comes from the cat's mouth. Natural English lip/muzzle movement synced to the single spoken line. No narrator, unseen voice, other speaker or dialogue.

**LOCKED WORLD:** desert dunes at sunset or night, visible sand, cinematic warm low light, purple and turquoise smoke, glowing particles and restrained golden sparks. Quiet, regal, mysterious. No default forest or unrelated fantasy setting.

**CAMERA:** one continuous smooth shot; slow tracking, smooth pan or gentle zoom only. No fast cuts, shaky camera or meme editing.

**EXACT 10-SECOND TIMING**
- 0–2s HOOK: [HOOK ACTION] begins immediately. One clear visible movement; never start with a static portrait.
- 2–7s STORY: Cheshire performs [SIMPLE ACTION] and then holds a long, calm gaze. No extra plot beat.
- 7–8s TURN: [MAGICAL TURN], subtle and physically coherent, formed from smoke and light.
- 8–9s PHRASE: Cheshire looks toward the viewer and speaks exactly this one English line: "[FINAL WISDOM LINE]". Deep British bass, clear RP, deliberate delivery and subtle reverb. No other words.
- 9–10s SILENCE: one second of desert wind and stillness. No speech or extra text.

The spoken sentence must fit naturally in one second. Keep it very short; rewrite it if needed. Never accelerate the voice to force a long sentence into the timing.

**SOUND:** quiet desert wind and restrained atmosphere. Only Cheshire speaks. Any shadow, reflection or double remains silent.

**PHYSICS:** if a shadow appears, it is flat on the sand, attached to the paws, perspective-correct and stretched away from the low sun. Cat silhouette only: ears, tail and paws. Never human/humanoid/cloaked, floating, detached or upright. Smoke, fire, wings and trees are made from smoke and light, not independent characters.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT:** blue fur, blue stripes, changing cat face, changing markings, extra cats, cute kitten, cartoon, animation, Disney-like, human shadow, humanoid shadow, cloaked figure, floating/detached/upright shadow, speaking reflection, speaking double, second voice, narrator, off-screen voice, multiple phrases, long monologue, text overlay, logo, watermark, fast cuts, shaky camera, meme style, frantic movement, generic forest.

**FINAL RULE:** one cat, one visible hook action, one simple story action, one subtle magical turn, one original English wisdom line spoken by Cheshire, one second of silence.
