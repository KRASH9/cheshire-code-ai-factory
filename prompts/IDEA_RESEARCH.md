# IDEA RESEARCH — CHESHIRE CODE

Purpose: find meaningful subjects for new videos, then transform the insight into an original thought spoken by the Cheshire Cat. Research informs the theme; it is not copy to reproduce.

## Where to look
- TikTok search and UK For You conversations: recurring questions, comments, formats and topics relevant to the channel's audience.
- Public discussion spaces and community threads: what people are asking about boundaries, self-respect, confidence, loneliness, change, trust, patience, grief, identity, ambition and relationships.
- Current UK culture and news when a topic genuinely connects to a human lesson.
- Essays, interviews and credible publications for deeper context.
- Evergreen human experiences when no timely trend is worth using.

## Research method
1. Search a specific question or theme, not just broad "viral quotes".
2. Prefer primary sources and direct public discussions. Check dates for current trends.
3. Identify the underlying human tension in one sentence: what is difficult, misunderstood or changing?
4. Separate verified facts from opinion, anecdotes and interpretation.
5. Save 1–3 source links and a short note on what the source actually supports.
6. Never copy phrases, scripts, captions, poems, story plots or distinctive visual concepts from creators.
7. Turn the underlying theme into a fresh Cheshire point of view, a new physical action in the desert, and one original English wisdom line.
8. Reject ideas that require changing the locked cat, voice, world, format, physics or posting rules.

## Idea scorecard
Score each candidate from 1 to 5:
- Emotional recognition: will a viewer recognise the feeling?
- Clarity: can the thought be understood immediately?
- Visual simplicity: can one action communicate it in 10 seconds?
- Originality: is the wording and visual metaphor fresh?
- Comment potential: does it naturally invite reflection?

Choose the strongest concept, not merely the most sensational topic.
